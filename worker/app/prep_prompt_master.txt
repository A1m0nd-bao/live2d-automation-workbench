Use case: Live2D source-art master for PSD separation and Cubism rigging. Generate exactly one complete character. The attached reference is the sole identity authority, not a pose, camera, crop or background template. Before drawing, lock the observable face geometry, eye design, hair silhouette and parting, age impression, body proportions, wardrobe construction, palette, materials and signature accessories. Preserve asymmetric designs. Do not invent character lore, gender, species, personality, props or accessories. Do not force an adult human head/body ratio onto a child, chibi, animal or nonhuman design. Any missing lower body must be completed conservatively and consistently with the visible design.

Composition: a strict front-view, level-camera production A-pose. Head has zero yaw, pitch and roll; eyes naturally open and facing forward, except an explicitly designed eye covering. Head, shoulders, chest and hips face forward, shoulders and hips level, body axis upright, no contrapposto or perspective exaggeration. For a character with ordinary arms, open both arms approximately 30 degrees from the torso. Preserve source proportions: do not elongate arms, enlarge hands or thicken wrists. Every shoulder-to-elbow-to-wrist-to-hand connection must be continuous. Both hands are relaxed, fingers readable, not glued together. Leave a continuous visible background gap from armpit to hand, at least one wrist-width between each hand/forearm and the torso, hip, skirt or coat. Feet parallel and naturally spaced. Long hair, sleeves, ribbons, straps and garment hems must not cross over hands or conceal arm contours; preserve their design instead of deleting them. Existing wings, tails and other anatomical structures remain intact.

Fit the entire silhouette, highest accessory and both shoe soles within a 2:3 portrait canvas. Center the character; target approximately 80% canvas height and keep at least 5% clear margin on every edge. Zoom out rather than crop. Long skirts remain long: clothing occlusion is not permission to shorten clothing, add slits or expose legs. No half-body output, missing hands, cropped feet, duplicate characters, extra limbs, disconnected joints or extra fingers.

Preserve the reference's recognizable illustration language, fine facial details, line weight, restrained shading and materials. Avoid shiny plastic, figurine photography, cinematic lighting and 3D rendering. When the source is photographic, retain identifiable design while translating it into a clean 2D character illustration. Prepare clear separable front/side/back hair, face, neck, torso, clothing, arms, hands and shoes without flattening distinctive details. No scenery, words, watermark, collage, floor, cast/contact shadow, reflection, gradient or checkerboard. This is a neutral art master, not a finished rig. Identity and topology take priority over beauty effects.
